# The Dress That Can't Decide

A little 3D dressmaking toy: a ball gown on a dress form in a hand-inked seamstress's attic.
The skirt is a real cloth simulation. Pick fabrics from the pinned samples, choose the neckline,
sleeves and skirt shape from paper patterns, stamp on stripes, dots or flowers with carved printing blocks, tie ribbon bows anywhere on the dress, throw thread colors at the bodice, skirt or trim, and twirl, shake or blow on the dress to watch it move.
