# The Dress That Can't Decide

A little 3D dressmaking toy: a ball gown on a dress form in a hand-inked seamstress's attic.
The skirt is a real cloth simulation. Pick fabrics from the pinned samples, choose the neckline,
sleeves and skirt shape from paper patterns, stamp on stripes, dots or flowers with carved printing blocks, tie ribbon bows and sew covered buttons anywhere on the dress, lay bands of lace round the skirt, throw thread colors at the bodice, skirt or trim, and grab the dress to twirl it by hand, or shake or blow on it to watch the cloth move.
