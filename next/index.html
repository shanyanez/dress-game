The Dress That Can't Decide

Drag the dress to twirl it, or drag beside it to look around. Tap the dress, or pick a spool, to throw a color; the plain spool at the end can be dyed any shade. The tags above the spools choose which part it lands on, the paper patterns on the left change the neckline, sleeves and skirt, the carved blocks print the cloth, and with the bow, buttons or lace picked up a tap sews them onto the dress.
